ABOUT SCADASK

Curiosity is
our starting point.

We’re an applied research studio working from inside the creative economy. Our work helps people understand change—and decide how to respond.

✳

CREATIVE THINKING. RIGOROUS RESEARCH.

A university’s perspective.
A studio’s approach.

Based at the Savannah College of Art and Design, SCADask works alongside the students entering the creative economy and the faculty teaching them. Our design-centered research examines the future of commerce, creativity, culture, and technology.

From original research for clients to internal research and development, we produce evidence that helps leaders make informed decisions.

WHAT WE DO

Questions become possibilities.

01 / ORIGINAL RESEARCH

Understand what’s changing.

Explore emerging behaviors, industry shifts, and the questions that matter to your organization.

02 / PUBLISHED PERSPECTIVES

Make insight accessible.

Reports translate research into clear perspectives on the forces shaping creative careers and industries.

03 / CONVERSATIONS & EXPERIMENTS

Bring ideas into practice.

Initiatives include SCAD’s annual AI Summit, the SCAD Signal podcast, and explorations like SplatGarden.
