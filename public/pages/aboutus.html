ABOUT THE STUDIO

Inside
SCADask.

Applied research at the Savannah College of Art and Design.

SCADask works from inside the creative economy, alongside the students entering it and the faculty teaching them. Our design-centered research examines the future of commerce, creativity, culture, and technology.

From original research for clients to internal research and development, we produce evidence that helps leaders make informed decisions.
