QS Back to School Summit

Research Reimagined:
AI, IP & the New Rules of Discovery

2:00 PM – 2:30 PM · New York City

Join SCADask Executive Director Munkhtsetseg Nandigjav with Dr. Maali Alabdulhafith and Sanjay Goel for a panel at the QS Back to School Summit about what AI means for future research ecosystems. The panel will explore topics like how AI-enabled research is evaluated to how research institutions can harness the power of AI to enhance discovery.
